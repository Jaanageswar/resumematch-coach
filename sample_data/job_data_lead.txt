Principal Machine Learning Engineer
Kestrel AI — Berlin (Remote within the EU)

ABOUT THE ROLE
Kestrel AI builds demand forecasting products for European retailers. We are
hiring a Principal ML Engineer to own our modelling platform end to end and to
set the technical direction for a team of six data scientists and ML engineers.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
- Set the technical direction for forecasting and recommendation models
- Own the full lifecycle: feature engineering, training, deployment, monitoring
- Lead the team of six and grow the engineers in it through review and mentoring
- Move batch models to real-time inference behind a low-latency serving layer
- Introduce a feature store so features are consistent in training and serving
- Build retrieval-augmented generation into our forecasting assistant, using a
  vector database over our product catalogue
- Scale distributed training and tuning with Ray
- Establish model governance and documentation for the EU AI Act

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
- 8+ years in applied machine learning, with production ownership
- Expert Python, with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Strong classical ML: scikit-learn, feature engineering, model evaluation
- Production MLOps: MLflow, model registries, reproducible training
- Orchestration with Airflow and transformations with dbt
- Distributed data processing with Spark
- Deployment on Kubernetes with infrastructure as code
- Experience leading and mentoring a team of engineers
- Rigorous experiment design and A/B testing

NICE TO HAVE
- LLMs in production: RAG, embeddings, vector databases, prompt evaluation
- A feature store such as Feast or Tecton
- Streaming ML on Kafka for real-time features
- Distributed training with Ray or Dask
- Google Cloud, in addition to or instead of AWS
- Snowflake as a warehouse
- Model governance under the EU AI Act

WHAT WE LOOK FOR
We care about models that ship and stay healthy. Strong candidates can describe
a model they took to production, how they knew it was working, and what they did
when it drifted. We value engineers who can explain a trade-off to a
non-technical stakeholder and who raise the standard of everyone around them.
